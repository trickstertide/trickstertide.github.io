Join the closed test.

Setsmith is in Google Play closed testing (early build). Free, no account, no ads. Android phones only. Use the same Google account for every step: the one on your Android phone. Do the steps in this order:

  1. Join the Google Group. Open the group, sign in if asked, and tap "Join group".
  2. Become a tester. Open the tester page and tap "Become a tester".
  3. Install from Google Play. Tap the Play link on that page, or search for Setsmith in Google Play. If it says "not available", wait a few minutes and try again.

The links work on Android phones only; they will not install anything on an iPhone. A different Google account is the most common reason for "not available".

Then lift as usual for two weeks and send bugs and ideas from Settings > Send feedback. Please stay opted in for the full two weeks; Google needs testers opted in continuously for 14 days.

Stuck? Email us from any address with your Google address and what you see on screen. The address is used only to help you get access. We are looking for lifters, especially Strong, Hevy or FitNotes users. Testers need Android 7.0 or newer; the app is portrait-only, so some tablets and Chromebooks will not be offered it on Google Play.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, free to use, no ads. No purchases in this build.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you email feedback. You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. Data stays on your phone until you export it.

Can I import from Strong or Hevy?

Yes, from their CSV export (Settings > Import from Strong or Hevy). Assisted, cardio and rest rows are skipped. Other apps (FitNotes etc.) are not supported yet.
