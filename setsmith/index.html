What it does

  • Weekly sets per muscle

    Hard sets against a target range, so you can see what is low.

  • Last time, then today's target

    Pre-filled, a few more reps first, then a little more weight.

  • 3D guides for 100+ lifts

    Step through each lift with the muscles it works. Custom exercises have none.

  • Weekly check-in

    What moved and what stalled.

  • Built for the gym

    Rest timer notification (allow notifications; can arrive a little late on some phones), edit finished workouts, body weight log with Undo.

  • Switch from Strong or Hevy

    Import their CSV export. Assisted, cardio and rest rows are skipped. Other apps (FitNotes etc.) are not supported yet.

No account, no ads, no analytics in this build. Data stays on your phone until you export it. This site sets no cookies and tracks no one.

How it works

  1. Pick a plan

    Start a workout from one of your plans.

  2. Log sets, see last time

    Each set shows last time and today's target, pre-filled.

  3. Check your weekly sets

    See hard sets per muscle against a target range.

Join the closed test

Setsmith is in Google Play closed testing (early build). The public join link is not ready yet, so ask for an invite by email.

  1. Email us from the Google account your Android phone uses. Request an invite
  2. We add your address to the test and send you a link. Open it on your phone, accept, install from Play.
  3. Lift as you normally do for a couple of weeks, send bugs and ideas from Settings > Send feedback.

Put in the email: the Gmail/Google address Play uses, phone model, the app you use now, lifts per week. We use it only to send the invite. Testers need an Android phone on Android 7.0 or newer; we are looking for lifters, especially Strong, Hevy or FitNotes users.

The app is portrait-only, so some tablets and Chromebooks will not be offered it on Google Play. iPhone: not yet.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, free to use, no ads. No purchases in this build.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you email feedback. You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. Data stays on your phone until you export it.

Can I import from Strong or Hevy?

Yes, from their CSV export (Settings > Import from Strong or Hevy). Assisted, cardio and rest rows are skipped. Other apps (FitNotes etc.) are not supported yet.
