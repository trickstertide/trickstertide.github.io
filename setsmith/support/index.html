Questions

Is it free?

Yes, free to use, no ads. No purchases in this build.

Do I need an account?

No.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you email feedback. You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. Data stays on your phone until you export it.

Can I import from Strong or Hevy?

Yes, from their CSV export (Settings > Import from Strong or Hevy). Assisted, cardio and rest rows are skipped. Other apps (FitNotes etc.) are not supported yet.

Which phones?

Android 7.0 or newer phones. The app is portrait-only, so some tablets and Chromebooks will not be offered it on Google Play. Compatibility shows on the Play page once you are invited.

iPhone?

Not yet. Android only for now; no date promised.

How do I join the test?

Request an invite by email (see Join the closed test); testers are added by email.

How do I report a bug?

Settings > Send feedback opens an email with your app version, Android version and phone model pre-filled. Or use the template on this page.

Why was my rest timer alert late?

Allow notifications for Setsmith. The alert can arrive a little late on some phones.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling Setsmith deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them.

What will it cost later?

Setsmith is free to use today, with no ads and no purchases in this build.

Is this medical advice?

No. Setsmith is a logging tool. Talk to a doctor or qualified coach about your health or training.
