Setsmith privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: your workouts stay on your phone. Setsmith has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Setsmith is a gym tracking app made by trickstertide, an independent developer. You can reach us at trickstertide@gmail.com. We are the controller of any personal data described in this policy.

What Setsmith stores

Your workouts, exercises, sets, body weight, notes and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not run a server for the app and we have no copy of your data.

What Setsmith sends out

Nothing. This version has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect or share your personal data, and we do not receive any information about how you use the app.

Things you can do that involve other services

  • Export a backup. Settings → Export backup creates a file on your phone. Where you save or send it (Drive, email, another app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Import a backup or a Strong or Hevy export. Setsmith reads the file you choose, on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Send feedback. This opens your email app with a message to us that already contains the app version, build number, Android version and phone model. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you do, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply and to improve the app. We delete them when you ask.
  • Links. Rows such as the privacy policy or a feedback form open in your browser. That website's own policy applies.
  • Android backup. Android may copy Setsmith's data to your Google account if you have Google backup switched on. That is controlled by Android and Google, not by Setsmith.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications and exact alarms, so the rest timer can tell you when your rest is over, even with the screen locked. These are generated on your phone.
  • Vibration, for the feel of logging a set.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because your training data never leaves your phone, you already have full control of it. Uninstalling Setsmith deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them. If you emailed us and want your message deleted, or want to see what we hold, write to trickstertide@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Setsmith is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add optional analytics or backup, we will update this page first, and the app will tell you before anything is sent. The date at the top shows the latest version.
